## Inventory maintenance

When a product, glossary term, article, white paper or case is published:

1. Add/update it in the website route or CMS source.
2. Confirm sitemap, llms.txt and agent-page generation update automatically.
3. Update hand-curated `agents.md` preferred URLs only when the page becomes a primary source.
4. Update this file by topic cluster, not by duplicating the article body.
5. Record claims, sources and review dates in the content system.
6. Check the Chinese twin and hreflang policy.

Temporary CMS pipeline test posts and non-public drafts must never enter this inventory, sitemap or public discovery files.
